// What We Do

One forum. Every corner of applied AI.

AISF exists to close the gap between the AI curriculum and what it actually takes to ship something real — through hands-on projects, focused workshops and a peer group that pushes each other forward.

/ 01

Open Collaboration

Cross-functional teams of developers, designers, and project leads working together on open source ideas and real-world neural pipelines.

/ 02

Career & Mentorship

Direct access to alumni in top tech companies, mock technical interviews, AI research paper reviews, and personalized portfolio coaching.

/ 03

Research & Sprints

Specialized student teams working on deep learning, computer vision, multimodal LLMs, and autonomous systems from first principles.

/ 04

Flagship Hackathons

Led by Code Apex 2.0, our nationwide 36-hour sprint where ambitious ideas transform into functioning AI prototypes under real pressure.

/ 05

Workshops & Bootcamps

Hands-on sessions taking members from theoretical equations to deployable neural architectures, guided by seniors and industry leaders.

/ 06

Open-Source Builds

Production-grade tools and shared repositories maintained together by the forum, engineered to establish VIT Pune as an AI powerhouse.
